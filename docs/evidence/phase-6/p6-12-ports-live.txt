### 8080 is not in the sample above, and the reason is a real property of the boot order:
### the Director logs `SpiralDB loaded` and only then reaches
### `Program.Main`'s `PlayerDatabase.Instance.Store` / `CreateEmbeddedDatabaseAccounts()`, which is
### what binds 8080 — and the harness stops the Director as soon as its line appears. So a
### single-boot sampler window sees 12500/12000/12333 (bound by `StartPatchServer`/`StartLoginServer`/
### `StartGameServers`, before or around the line) and not 8080.
###
### 8080 attributed live, by ancestry, is in `p6-12-down-ledger.txt`: a harness run whose process was
### killed by a broken pipe left the Director alive, and the next `probe` reported
###   `8080  imlight  owned  1801139  port 8080 answers from pid 1801139
###    (dotnet --fx-version 10.0.12 /…/tools/.imlight-run/RavenDBServer/Raven.Server…)`
### — 1801139 is the RavenDB child, and the ledger holds its *parent* (the Director), so the
### match is by ancestry. That is exactly why `classifyPort` walks `/proc/<pid>/stat`'s ppid chain.
