## 3. Measured corpus facts this story rests on

The corpus was surveyed before implementation and re-derived independently in §5. Every node — the
`RequirementList` wrapper included — is polymorphic and carries `m_applyNOT` + `m_operator`.

- 322 quest files hold **631 requirement nodes**: 314 wrappers (of which **7 are untyped**) and
  317 leaves. `m_requirements` is non-empty in **314** and `null` in **8**.
- Leaf classes in a quest's own tree: **`ReqHasQuest` 277, `ReqHasEntry` 40** only.
  **`ReqHasGoal`/`ReqEntryValue` occur 0 times corpus-wide** (the spec's "do NOT implement" is
  corpus-consistent). `ReqSchoolOfFocus` (63) and `ReqIsSchool` (2) live **only** in DropTable item
  requirements, and quest-side they appear **only inside result objects**
  (`m_endResults.m_results[].m_requirements` 14×, `m_goals[].m_completeResults.m_results[].m_requirements` 7×).
- **Five distinct key orders** exist and all five are preserved: (a) 277 × `$type,m_applyNOT,m_operator,m_requirements`;
  (b) 277 × `$type,m_applyNOT,m_operator,m_questName`; (c) 40 ×
  `$type,m_entryName,m_displayName,m_isQuestRegistry,m_questName,m_applyNOT,m_operator`;
  (d) 30 × `$type,m_requirements,m_applyNOT,m_operator`; (e) 7 × `m_requirements,m_applyNOT,m_operator` (untyped).
- **`ReqHasEntry` carries two keys the domain reference omits**: `m_displayName` (`null` in 40/40)
  and `m_isQuestRegistry` (`true` in 39, `false` in 1). They are modelled, editable, defaulted from
  the corpus, and surfaced with help text that says exactly that.
- **`m_operator` is `ROP_AND` in 631/631** and `m_applyNOT` is `true` in **exactly 1** node.
  Depth is 0 or 1 only — **the corpus never nests a group inside a group**, and child counts are
  1 → 312, 2 → 1, 3 → 1 (so no empty list exists).
- The three other slots the tab owns are all corpus-empty: `m_prepRequirements` `null` in
  322/322, `m_pruneRequirements` present-but-`null` in 322/322, `m_goalRequirements` `null` in
  all **772** goals.

Consequence, recorded honestly: **`ROP_OR`, a nested group and the AC1 cross-type mix are
fixture-only**, while the two leaf classes the AC names as `OR`-ed appear in the corpus only in
DropTable items and inside results — never together with `ReqHasQuest`, and never under `ROP_OR`.
The model header, the unit fixture and the tier-1 spec header each say so, and no comment claims
the corpus exercises them. The AC1 expectation text is hand-written for the same reason.

**Two of my expectations were wrong and the model was right both times**, recorded in that file: I

`border-zinc-800`. The spec asks for a coloured **left** border, so the model's reading is the more
precise one — and tier-2 settled it by measuring the **computed** left border, which is independent
of any class name.

## 7. Decision D62 (recorded in `docs/plan-overview.md`)

- **Four slots, not two.** `lib/quest-info.ts` L194-196 labels `m_requirements`,
  `m_prepRequirements` and `m_pruneRequirements` as owned by the Requirements tab, and
  `lib/quest-goals.ts` L406 labels `m_goalRequirements` the same way — inventory labels that D60(d)
  also assigned to task 3.6. Wiring only the two the AC names would have left three of those owner
  labels false, so all four are wired; the two silently-empty ones are labelled as synthetic
  territory in the UI, and dropping the extras is confined to the 145-line host.
- **A class change is a replacement** in the new class's canonical order — the one place an existing
  node is rebuilt. Merging two classes' field sets would either keep foreign keys or silently drop
  them, and carrying values across classes would be a value guess.
- **A new group starts with one default condition**, because the corpus never carries an empty
  `m_requirements` array.
- **`ReqHasEntry`'s two undocumented keys are modelled** and default from the corpus
  (`m_displayName: null` 40/40, `m_isQuestRegistry: true` 39/40) rather than being dropped as
  "not in the spec" — a key that exists in 40 of 40 real nodes is data, not noise.
- **A group's own `m_applyNOT` is preserved but not editable** (the AC and the ASCII put NOT on a
  leaf; all 314 wrappers are `false` and the single `true` node is a leaf). Recorded as a stated UI
  limitation, not a silent one.
- **Per-side border classes** (`border-l-*`) so the spec's colour cannot be lost to CSS order.

  not to disk.

- The tree's validation (the domain reference's reachability-style rules) is not part of this story;
  the AC does not name it.
