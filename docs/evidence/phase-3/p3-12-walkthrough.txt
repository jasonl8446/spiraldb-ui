## A recurring, already-documented console line

Every page in this pass logged exactly one console error:
`Failed to load resource: the server responded with a status of 404 (Not Found) @ /api/names/npcs/0`.
That is the **sentinel `0`** in `m_walkAwayNpcTemplateID` reaching the friendly-name lookup — the
displayed behaviour is correct (the raw `0` is shown and never rewritten, the D59(d) miss path), it
was recorded in p3-08 as a Phase-5 polish candidate, and it appears on every dialog-bearing quest
because `0` means "no walk-away NPC" in 1,675 of 1,706 real entries (D64f).

  product finding**. Re-measured as `"▾Basic=true"` with the other four collapsed, on both Q1 and Q3.
