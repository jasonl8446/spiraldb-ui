8. What the tier-1 missing waits had hidden (D60(i))
----------------------------------------------------
The two reorder specs and `copyPanelDocument` were failing on timing, not on behaviour: the
product reorders correctly in both sensors, and the reorder is observable in the *product's
own* signals (dnd-kit's live region + the card order). The specs now await those signals
instead of fixed delays; the zone dropdown's search box is addressed by
`getByRole('combobox', { name: 'Search zones' })` because `getByLabel` does not resolve that
input even though it carries the matching `aria-label`.
